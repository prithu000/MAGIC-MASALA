1. Products & Inventory (मसाले और प्रोडक्ट्स कैसे जोड़ें)

Shop page par dikhne wale masalon ki complete details yahan se set hoti hain.

All Products Open Karein arrow_forward

payments MRP vs Selling Price (कीमत और डिस्काउंट)

  • MRP (Maximum Retail Price): Ye masale ki print rate hai (jaise ₹120).
  • Price (Selling Price): Jis discount rate par aap customer ko bechna chahte hain (jaise ₹99).
  • Automatic Discount Tag: Website khud calculate karke badge dikhayegi jaise "18% OFF" aur MRP par cut lagayegi.

layers Product Variants (100g, 200g, 500g Packs)

  • Har product ke page ke neeche "Product Variants" inline hota hai.
  • Agar masale ke multiple pack sizes hain (100g pack, 250g pack), to alag product banane ki zaroorat nahi hai.
  • Bas variant me weight (e.g. 200g), MRP, Price aur Stock daal dein. Customer product page par dropdown se select kar sakega.

format_list_bulleted Accordion Tabs (स्वाद, सामग्री और फायदे)

  • Taste Profile: Masale ka taste kaisa hai (jaise "Zesty, Warm, Pungent").
  • Ingredients: Isme kaun-kaun se masale hain (e.g., Cumin, Coriander, Black Pepper).
  • Uses / How to Use: Kaunsi dishes me kab daalna hai (e.g., "Add 1 tsp during tempering").
  • Shelf Life: Kitne mahine tak kharab nahi hoga (e.g., "12 Months from packaging").

star Featured & Bestseller Flags

  • Is Featured / Bestseller Checkbox: Isko tick karne par ye masala Homepage ke "Bestseller" section me aayega.
  • Homepage Limit: Homepage par automatically top 4 bestsellers dikhte hain, aur baaki sab "View All Spices" par click karke shop me dikhte hain.

2. Combos & Build-Your-Own Box (कॉम्बो पैक्स)

Fixed combos aur 10 masalon ka custom box offer yahan se control hota hai.

Fixed Combos Open Karein arrow_forward

home Fixed Combo: Homepage Par Kaunsa Dikhana Hai?

Fixed Combo edit karte waqt ek checkbox aata hai: "Featured On Homepage".

Rule: Aap jis combo par "Featured On Homepage" tick karenge, wahi ek combo Homepage par "Featured Spice Combo" card bankar dikhega jisme direct Add-To-Cart button hoga. Baaki saare combos /combos/ page par dikhenge!

tune Build Your Own Combo (10 for ₹999 Box)

Customer apne manpasand masale chun kar box bana sakte hain. Settings yahan se badlein:

  • Required Items: Kitne masale chunne hain (default: 10 items).
  • Fixed Price: Box ki flat price kya hogi (default: ₹999).
  • Max Units per Spice: Ek hi masale ke maximum kitne pack le sakta hai (default: 2 pack). Isse customer saare mehnge masale ek saath nahi le sakta.

swap_vert "Display Order" (प्रदर्शन क्रम) Ka Kya Matlab Hai?

Admin panel me har cheez (Slide, Category, Reel) me Display Order field hota hai.

  • Number 1: Sabse pehle number par aayega (First slide).
  • Number 2: Dusre number par aayega.

link Slide Link URL & Text

  • Is Active: Kisi slide ko temporarily band karna ho to bas Is Active uncheck kar dein, slide delete karne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

4. Order Processing & COD Advance (ऑर्डर कैसे प्रोसेस करें)

Customer ke aane wale naye orders aur COD advance check karne ka tarika.

All Orders Open Karein arrow_forward

percent 10% COD Advance Policy

Fake COD orders se bachne ke liye store par 10% Advance mandatory hai.

  • Customer order karte waqt 10% online pay karta hai (Razorpay UPI / Card).
  • Baaki 90% amount delivery ke time Cash On Delivery pay hota hai.
  • Order list me dekhein: Advance Paid: Yes / No clear green badge me dikhta hai.

local_shipping Order Status Workflow (स्टेप बाय स्टेप)

1. Placed Customer ne checkout kiya.
2. Processing Advance confirm ho gaya, godown me masala pack ho raha hai.
3. Shipped Courier ko de diya (Tracking details update kar dein).
4. Delivered Customer ko packet mil gaya.

5. Customer Carts Tracking (कस्टमर कार्ट्स और बैग डिटेल्स)

Live customers aur guests ne kya masale cart me add kiye hain unki puri detail yahan dekhein.

All Carts Open Karein arrow_forward

visibility Cart Details & Masala Breakdown

Admin panel me ab har customer ki live cart visible hai:

  • User vs Guest: Login user ka naam aur email dikhta hai; guest ke liye session id dikhti hai.
  • Items & Cart Total: Cart me kitne items hain aur kul kitne rupaye (₹) ka cart value hai.
  • Custom 10-Masala Box Breakdown: Agar customer ne Build Your Own Box add kiya hai to box ke andar chune gaye sabhi 10 masalon ki puri list saaf dikhegi!

notifications_active Abandoned Carts & Actions

Cart status automatically classify hota hai:

  • Active / Recent: P Pichle 24 ghante me customer ne cart me badlaav kiya hai.
  • Abandoned: 24 ghante se zyada samay ho gaya aur order place nahi hua.
  • Admin Action: Cart list me checkbox select karke "Send abandoned cart reminder email" action se reminder bhej sakte hain.
